    Message to the supplier

    As of 21 November 2026. Orientation only, not legal advice. The list applies general rules to the values you enter. Local rules are built in for the EU, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Türkiye, the United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, China, Japan, South Korea, India and Australia. The EU rules are the most detailed; for the other countries the list covers the main duties. For every other destination it shows the general checks and what to confirm locally. It cannot see the restrictions of a specific carrier, ship, flight or port, and it does not check your product against the dangerous goods list. The shipper stays responsible for the classification. Label languages follow national rules, so confirm them for countries with more than one official language. The UK rules shown are those of Great Britain; Northern Ireland follows EU rules.
